01 Ingredients

Every ingredient earns its place.

Six ingredients, each one chosen for a job it can actually do. Read the label — everything in the bottle is there for a reason, and everything we left out is listed below.

"We formulate the way a cook writes a recipe: few ingredients, each one doing real work."

AURELLE formulas are built in small batches around cold-pressed plant oils and well-studied actives. We use concentrations that do the work, not token amounts for the label.

Every ingredient is named in plain language on the product page and on the bottle. If you cannot pronounce it, you can still look it up — the list below tells you what it is and what it does.

02 The library

Six ingredients. No secrets.

01

Rosehip oil

Rosa canina seed oil

Cold-pressed from rosehip seeds, rosehip oil is rich in linoleic acid and provitamin A. It supports an even-looking tone and a smoother feel, and it absorbs easily without sitting heavy on the skin.

In: Lumière Renewal Serum

02

Squalane

Squalane, from olive

A lightweight emollient that mimics the skin's own lipids. It locks in moisture without heaviness or shine, and it layers well under richer creams and over water-based formulas.

In: Lumière Renewal Serum, Crème Velours, Huile Nettoyante Cleansing Oil

03

Niacinamide

Vitamin B3

Niacinamide supports the skin barrier, helps tone look more even, and calms the look of redness. It is well tolerated by most skin types, which is why it sits at the centre of two of our formulas.

In: Lumière Renewal Serum, Éclat Eye Concentrate

04

Hyaluronic acid

Sodium hyaluronate

A humectant that draws water into the skin. It gives a plumped, hydrated look that lasts through the day, and it works quietly underneath oils and creams rather than competing with them.

In: Crème Velours, Éclat Eye Concentrate

05

Green tea

Camellia sinensis leaf extract

Green tea leaf extract is rich in antioxidant polyphenols. It helps skin look calm and rested, especially after cleansing, when skin is most in need of something gentle.

In: Huile Nettoyante Cleansing Oil, Éclat Eye Concentrate

06

Shea butter

Butyrospermum parkii butter

A rich plant butter that softens dry skin and seals in moisture. It is the reason Crème Velours feels cushiony, never greasy — the butter does the work, and the formula gets out of its way.

In: Crème Velours

03 What we leave out

Formulation choices, not accusations.

These are our own choices for our own formulas. Other brands make different ones, and that is their business.

  • Fragrance

    We use no added fragrance, natural or synthetic. What you smell is the plant oils themselves.

  • Parabens

    We preserve with gentler systems chosen for each formula, and we test every batch before it ships.

  • Sulfates

    Our cleanser is oil-based, so it never needed a sulfate to foam. It rinses clean on its own.

  • Mineral oil

    We use plant oils and butters instead. They cost more; they also do more.

  • Synthetic dyes

    The amber of the serum and the cream of the jar are the colours of the ingredients. Nothing is added for looks.
