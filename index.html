01 Manifesto

We make less, on purpose. Four formulas. Six ingredients. Nothing hidden.

Most skincare asks you to trust a label you cannot read. AURELLE does the opposite: every formula is built around a short list of ingredients, each one named, each one doing a job.

We make in small batches because plant oils are living things — cold-pressed, volatile, and best used fresh. When a harvest is short, the batch is short. We do not top it up with substitutes, and we do not stretch a good formula into ten mediocre ones.

  • i.Small batches, numbered by run. When a batch sells out, it sells out.
  • ii.Every ingredient named on the label. If you cannot pronounce it, you can look it up on the ingredients page.
  • iii.No fragrance, ever. What you smell is the plant oils themselves.

Guide

How to layer a serum so it actually absorbs

Read the guide

Serum works best on damp skin. After cleansing, leave your face slightly wet — water on the surface helps humectants like hyaluronic acid pull moisture in instead of sitting on top.

Press, don't rub. Rubbing pushes product onto your hands; pressing puts it on your face. Give each layer a minute before the next one, and use less than you think — three to four drops covers a whole face.

Ingredients

What niacinamide does, in plain language

Read the guide

Niacinamide is vitamin B3. It supports the skin barrier — the wall that keeps water in and irritants out — and it helps tone look more even over weeks, not overnight.

It plays well with almost everything, including vitamin C and retinol, and most skin types tolerate it daily. If a product lists it near the top of the ingredients, that is a good sign it is there to work, not to decorate the label.
